Privacy Policy

Invoice Maker Pro  ·  Last updated: September 11, 2026

Summary

Invoice Maker Pro is a local-first invoice generator for Android. Your invoices, clients, and catalog stay on your device. We run no servers and share nothing with advertisers. Two kinds of data leave your phone: (a) purchase verification with our subscription providers, and (b) anonymous crash reports that help us fix bugs (see Sections 3–5).

1. Information we collect

The App requires no account and has no servers. We do not collect, transmit, or store any of the following on our behalf:

  • Invoice, client, or catalog contents
  • Your name, email, or contact details
  • Location

All app data is stored locally in SQLite on your device and never leaves it unless you export or share a file yourself (invoice PDF, backup file).

Automatically collected (see Section 4): crash reports and usage analytics (release builds only). Crash reports include stack traces, device model, OS version, and app version. Usage analytics include app interactions (for example, an invoice was created or the paywall was viewed), screen names, a random app-install identifier, device model, OS version, app version, and approximate (country-level) location derived from IP address. Neither crash reports nor analytics ever include your invoice numbers, amounts, client or business details, or item text.

2. How we use information

Because the App is local-first, we use no personal data beyond what is strictly required to operate the features you choose to use:

  • PDF generation: to generate invoices, the App reads the invoice data you enter on your device.
  • Subscription entitlement: to unlock Pro features, the App checks your purchase status with the payment processors below.
  • Crash diagnostics: to fix stability bugs, release builds send anonymous crash reports as described below.

3. Payments and purchases

Pro is an optional subscription ("1 month", "6 months", and "1 year" plans). Purchases are processed by Google Play and managed by RevenueCat. We do not see or store your credit card or other payment details.

RevenueCat's handling of data is governed by its privacy policy. Google's handling of purchase data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Third parties

Provider Purpose Data involved
Google Play Subscription billing and payments Purchase identifiers, transaction details
RevenueCat Subscription verification and entitlements Purchase identifiers and app state
Firebase Crashlytics (Google) Crash diagnostics, release builds only Crash logs, device model/OS, app version — never invoice or client contents
Firebase Analytics (Google) Aggregate product usage and paywall conversion, release builds only App interaction events, screen names, app-install identifier, device model/OS, app version, approximate location — never invoice or client contents

No advertising SDKs. Analytics is used only to understand aggregate product usage and is not used for advertising, and nothing is sold or shared with advertisers.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We do not share the contents of your invoices with any third party. Data leaves your device only to the providers above, and only as needed for a purchase you initiate, a crash report from a release build, or aggregate usage analytics from a release build.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • App data: your invoices, clients, and catalog stay on your device until you delete them. Deleting an item, or uninstalling the App, removes it permanently.
  • Purchase records: Google Play retains your purchase history according to its own policies. You can cancel, renew, or delete purchases through your Google Play account.
  • Crash reports: retained by Google per Firebase policies; they contain no invoice or client data.
  • Usage analytics: retained by Google per Firebase policies, typically for up to 14 months; it contains no invoice or client data.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your region) and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your data: delete individual invoices, clients, or catalog items in the app, or uninstall the app to remove all local data.
  • Manage subscriptions: cancel or manage your subscription at any time through your Google Play account.
  • Crash reporting: release builds include crash reporting; uninstalling the app stops it. There is no in-app toggle.
  • Data protection rights: depending on your region (e.g. GDPR, CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. Contact us using the details below.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes takes effect constitutes acceptance.
